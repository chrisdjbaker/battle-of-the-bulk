HALLOWEEN BATTLE OF THE BULK — TOURNAMENT HUB
==============================================

HOW TO OPEN
  Double-click "Battle of the Bulk.html". It opens in your web browser.
  No internet needed, nothing to install. Chrome or Edge works best.

BEFORE THE EVENT
  1. Settings -> fill in the date, start time and any notes/rules.
  2. Kits -> rename any kit by clicking its name, or click "Photo"
     to upload a picture for it. You can also add or remove kits.
  3. Contestants -> add each player as they sign up and pay.

ON THE NIGHT
  * Kit Draw -> click "Roll the Bones". With "Random contestant" picked,
    it picks WHO draws next, then spins the kits and stops on one.
    Click "Undo this draw" if you need a do-over.
  * Bracket -> choose a format and click "Begin the Battle".
    Click any match to say who won. You can fix mistakes anytime.
  * Results -> champion and final standings.

TV / PROJECTOR
  Click "TV display" at the top right. A second window opens with no
  edit buttons. Drag it to the TV and click "Fullscreen".
  It updates live as you enter results, and plays the kit draw spin too.

SAVING (IMPORTANT)
  Everything saves automatically, but ONLY inside the browser on THIS
  computer. To be safe, or to move to another computer:
    Settings -> "Save backup file", then copy that file onto this USB stick.
    On the other computer: Settings -> "Load backup file".
  Use the same browser on the same computer throughout the event.

NEXT TOURNAMENT
  Settings -> "Clear contestants & tournament" keeps your kits and
  settings and wipes the players. Change the event name, kits, prices
  and posters for the new event.
